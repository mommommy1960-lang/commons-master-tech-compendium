# 💰 THE MAYA MODE MONETIZATION ENGINE

**Non-Predatory, Commons-Aligned Revenue System**  
**Ethical Commercialization Strategy**

---

## PHILOSOPHY

Maya Mode represents the Commons Initiative's commitment to **sustainable funding without exploitation**. This is not venture capital extraction. This is not growth-at-all-costs. This is not surveillance capitalism.

**Core Principles:**
1. **Zero AI exploitation** — No selling user data, no dark patterns, no manipulation
2. **Zero DRM** — No digital locks, no planned obsolescence, no vendor lock-in
3. **Zero consumer lock-in** — Open standards, interoperability, freedom to leave
4. **100% transparent supply chain** — Ethical sourcing, fair labor, environmental responsibility
5. **Community wealth building** — Profits reinvested in Commons network, not extracted

**Named "Maya Mode" in honor of:**  
Dr. Maya Angelou's principle: "Do the best you can until you know better. Then when you know better, do better."

We know better than predatory tech business models. So we do better.

## REVENUE PILLARS

### PILLAR 1: Hardware Sales

**Model:** Direct sales of Commons-designed hardware at transparent pricing

**Products:**
- PFHMS Smart Armor Tiles (individual or vest-scale kits)
- CALEXS XR Pods (turnkey installations)
- MPRS Navigation Robots (research/commercial units)
- CRTS Multi-Material Printers (makerspace/lab editions)
- BRL Soft-Limb Segments (research kits)
- Aurora Mini (specialized labs only)

**Pricing Strategy:**
```
Unit Cost = Materials + Fabrication + QA + Shipping + Margin

Margin Tiers:
- Educational/Nonprofit: 15% (covers support costs)
- Small Business/Startup: 25% (fair market rate)
- Large Corporation: 40% (subsidizes educational sales)
- Government: 30% (public sector pricing)
```

**Example: PFHMS Tile Pricing**
```
Material cost: $86
Fabrication (at scale): $20
QA/Testing: $8
Packaging/Shipping: $6
Base cost: $120

Educational price: $138 (15% margin = $18)
Small business price: $150 (25% margin = $30)
Corporate price: $168 (40% margin = $48)

Volume discounts:
10-49 units: 10% off
50-99 units: 15% off
100+ units: 20% off + custom pricing
```

**Projected Year 1 Revenue:**
- PFHMS Tiles: 500 units × $150 avg = $75,000
- CALEXS Pods: 5 units × $8,000 avg = $40,000
- MPRS Robots: 10 units × $2,500 avg = $25,000
- CRTS Cells: 3 units × $4,000 avg = $12,000
- **Total Hardware Sales:** $152,000

**Year 1-3 Growth Trajectory:**
- Year 1: $150K
- Year 2: $400K (more sites, word-of-mouth)
- Year 3: $900K (national network effect)

## ETHICAL SAFEGUARDS

### Revenue Source Screening

All potential revenue must pass through ethical review:

**Automatic REJECT:**
- Weapons manufacturing or sale
- Surveillance capitalism (selling user data)
- Exploitative labor (sweatshops, child labor)
- Environmental destruction (fossil fuel extraction, deforestation)
- Predatory lending or gambling
- Tobacco or addictive substances

**Requires Ethics Board Review:**
- Government contracts (case-by-case, must serve public good)
- Large corporations (audit for labor practices, environmental impact)
- Any use case with potential for abuse

**Pre-Approved:**
- Educational institutions (public, nonprofit, mission-aligned private)
- Nonprofits with aligned missions
- Community organizations
- Small businesses (verified ethical practices)
- Individuals (personal use, research)

### Profit Allocation

**No dividends to shareholders** (Commons is nonprofit or public benefit corp)

**Profit reinvestment priorities:**
1. **Reserve fund** (6 months operating costs) — 30% of profit until target met
2. **Expansion** (new sites, equipment) — 40% of profit
3. **R&D** (new systems, improvements) — 20% of profit
4. **Scholarships** (low-income participants) — 10% of profit

**Staff compensation:**
- Competitive salaries (at market rate for region)
- NO executive bonuses based on profit
- Profit-sharing: Annual bonus pool (5% of profit) distributed equally to all staff

### Transparency

**Public reporting:**
- Quarterly revenue reports (by pillar, anonymized)
- Annual financial statements (audited)
- Salary ranges published (by role, not individual)
- Grant awards listed (funder, amount, purpose)
- Major contracts disclosed (partner, value, terms)

**Community accountability:**
- Annual town hall (in-person + virtual)
- Open Q&A with leadership
- Feedback mechanisms (surveys, comment periods)
- Advisory board (community representatives)

## COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

### How Commons is Different

**vs. Traditional Startups:**
- They: Maximize profit for investors
- Commons: Maximize impact for communities

**vs. Nonprofits:**
- They: Dependent on grants and donations
- Commons: Diversified revenue, partially self-sustaining

**vs. Open Source Projects:**
- They: Often volunteer-driven, inconsistent funding

**vs. Academic Labs:**
