# Civic Continuum — Permanent Accountability Covenant
Adopted as a founding policy declaration: 2026-10-19
Status: Public founding commitment. This document is not by itself an executed corporate charter, contract, or legally entrenched bylaw.

## Our motto
**The standards we demand of power are the standards we must demonstrate ourselves.**

## Founding clause — No exceptions to accountability
Civic Continuum commits, for as long as the organization exists, to apply transparency, fairness, human dignity, consent, truthfulness, evidence, nonviolence, and meaningful accountability to its own decisions and operations, not merely to the institutions it scrutinizes.

No founder, executive, investor, partner, software agent, contractor, or governing body is exempt. Revenue, prestige, political convenience, growth, competitive pressure, and claims of good intentions cannot excuse deception, concealed harm, exploitation, retaliation, or unreviewable authority.

We will not present untested research as proven, unverified security as certified, or commitments as completed work. We will preserve negative findings and promptly correct material errors.

## Integrity in practice
1. **Public standards:** Publish understandable policies, decision criteria, limitations, and meaningful performance evidence, while protecting personal privacy and legitimate security-sensitive information.
2. **Reviewable authority:** Record who authorized consequential actions, under what scope, and when authority expires or must be reconsidered.
3. **Independent challenge:** Enable good-faith criticism, appeal, reporting, and independent review without retaliation.
4. **Repair:** Investigate substantiated harms, document corrective actions, and verify remedies rather than relying on apologies.
5. **No hidden exemptions:** Any emergency exception must have documented reasons, named responsibility, time limits, oversight, and a review trail; no exception may suspend the core commitments of honesty, human dignity, or accountability.
6. **Protection against capture:** Disclose material conflicts, prohibit self-dealing, and ensure financial or institutional leverage cannot secretly override the mission.
7. **Accountability for AI:** Require appropriate consent, human oversight, traceability, and stop mechanisms for consequential AI systems, including Aurora.
8. **Public corrections:** Keep a dated history of material policy changes, errors, and remediation, with necessary privacy protections.

## Permanence and enforcement
This covenant is intended to remain a standing commitment throughout the organization's life. Any future governance body should preserve its substantive principles and must not quietly weaken them. Changes for clarity, stronger safeguards, or legal compliance should be public, dated, reasoned, and subject to independent scrutiny.

A promise of permanence is not technically or legally irreversible merely because it appears in a GitHub file. For binding protection, adopt appropriate bylaws or operating-agreement provisions, define amendment thresholds and independent oversight, and obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review. This covenant records the intended standard now; enforcement mechanisms remain to be implemented.

## Practical standard
Good intentions are not evidence of good outcomes. **We measure whether the commitments work in practice.** When our actions fall short, the obligation is disclosure, correction, repair, and demonstrable prevention of recurrence, not a private exemption.

## Anti-obsolescence and honest growth (founding commitment)
We will not deliberately sabotage, cripple, shorten the useful life of, or degrade an otherwise safe and functioning product merely to compel repeat purchases or preserve revenue. We will prioritize reliability, repairability, maintainability, accurate lifespan disclosures and customer control, subject to real safety, security, legal and technical constraints. When an offering reaches maturity, we will seek growth through genuine innovation, new useful products, support and improvements rather than manufactured failure. This commitment applies to software updates, hardware design, subscription practices and replacement-part policies. Document justified deprecations and provide reasonable transition paths. An independent review must examine allegations of intentional premature obsolescence.
