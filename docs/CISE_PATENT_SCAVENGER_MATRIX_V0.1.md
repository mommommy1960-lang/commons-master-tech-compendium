# CISE Bicycle Research — Public Status

**Date:** September 26, 2026  
**Status:** Concept research and controlled validation

Civic Continuum is evaluating a resilient electric-bicycle mobility concept known as CISE.

The current work includes:

- prior-art and patent-landscape research;
- energy-efficiency and safety modeling;
- accessibility, stability, and emergency-mobility research;
- staged engineering validation with explicit stop gates; and
- independent legal, mechanical, electrical, and road-safety review before physical deployment.

The project is not presented as a completed vehicle, validated self-balancing system, or commercially available product. Public statements will report goals, evidence, test status, and safety boundaries without publishing confidential implementation details.

## Disclosure boundary

The following remain non-public during development:

- component arrangements and integration methods;
- dimensions, materials, tolerances, and performance thresholds;
- electrical architecture, control logic, algorithms, and source code;
- wiring, fabrication, assembly, calibration, and operating procedures;
- unpublished test data, failure analyses, and design-around work; and
- patent-claim mapping or other attorney-review material.

Any future technical release will be deliberate, versioned, reviewed for safety and intellectual-property implications, and limited to what Civic Continuum has chosen to disclose.

**Research boundary:** No public-road, range, stabilization, emergency-performance, patentability, or freedom-to-operate claim is made by this notice.
