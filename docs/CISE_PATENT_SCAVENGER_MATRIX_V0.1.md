**Status:** Research and concept design only; no prototype or freedom-to-operate opinion

## Objective

Identify expired or lapsed patent disclosures that may supply useful engineering building blocks for the CISE electric-bike system, while excluding active claims and placing every recovered mechanism behind safety and performance gates.

## CISE v0.2 scavenger architecture

1. **Primary recovery path:** compatible regenerative hub or generator captures braking and controlled downhill energy.
2. **Power conditioning:** rectifier or motor controller feeds overcurrent, overvoltage, and thermal protection.
3. **Burst buffer:** a supercapacitor bank accepts short regenerative peaks.
4. **Protected storage:** the controller transfers energy to a removable battery at a safe rate.
5. **Reserve policy:** lighting, communications, and safety electronics retain priority over propulsion assistance.
6. **Solar supplement:** deployable or integrated photovoltaic surfaces provide supplementary charging, especially while parked.
7. **Experimental suspension harvester:** isolated test channel; retained only if measured lifetime energy exceeds its mass, drag, and complexity penalties.
8. **Optional mechanical reserve:** elastic storage remains a separate research branch and must never interfere with ordinary brakes.
9. **Gyroscopic stabilization:** separate safety subsystem with independent shutdown, containment, and mechanical fallback; no adoption of the active patent's specific control arrangement.

## Mandatory test gates

### Gate 0 — Legal-family check

- Verify each jurisdiction and every continuation, divisional, utility-model, and national-family member.
- Confirm maintenance and revival records through the relevant official patent office.
- Compare live independent claims against the proposed CISE arrangement before fabrication or sale.

### Gate 1 — Energy ledger

- Measure mechanical input, electrical output, stored energy, conversion loss, and added drag.
- Reject any harvester that produces less useful energy than its mass and resistance cost justify.

### Gate 2 — Fault isolation

- Every experimental source requires a fuse, disconnect, temperature limit, overvoltage limit, and fail-safe coast mode.
- Mechanical brakes remain fully functional and independent.

### Gate 3 — Rotating-energy containment

- No rider-mounted flywheel or control-moment gyro testing before overspeed, imbalance, bearing-failure, rotor-burst, and enclosure tests behind a barrier.
- Add vibration sensing and automatic shutdown before any mobile test.

### Gate 4 — Bicycle integration

- Verify steering clearance, brake performance, structural loads, wiring retention, water resistance, and safe center of gravity.
- Begin on a stationary stand, then a closed low-speed course; no public-road testing until qualified review.

## Public-safe description

CISE is a concept-stage electric bicycle mobility platform combining protected energy management, recoverable braking and downhill energy, supplementary solar charging, prioritized safety and communications power, and a separately validated stabilization system. Historical expired-patent disclosures are being evaluated only as engineering references. No claim is made that CISE is the first solar bicycle, the first regenerative bicycle, or a validated self-balancing vehicle.

## Immediate engineering decision

Advance the suspension-energy concept to calculation and bench measurement. Keep elastic braking storage optional. Keep all flywheel and gyroscopic hardware behind a containment-and-claim-review gate. Build the first physical Energy Pod prototype without propulsion or rider-mounted high-speed rotors: generator input, supercapacitor buffer, protected low-voltage storage, lighting, communications power, data logging, and emergency USB output.
